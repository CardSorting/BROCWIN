# Support and questions

Start with the [walkthrough](docs/walkthrough.md), [research publications](publications/2026-10-11-v1.0.1/README.md) and [frozen closeout](wiki/closeout.md). The project is a research archive; no installation-support SLA, playable-game guarantee or managed service is offered.

Once a public repository has an assigned owner and enabled issue tracker, use its documentation-question or evidence-correction form for ordinary questions. Its URL is not configured in this local preparation. Sensitive security or conduct concerns require a verified private channel as described in SECURITY.md and CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md.

Include the document/file path, edition or hash, exact question and what you already inspected. Omit credentials, account identifiers and raw private logs. Missing original inputs in a public checkout are expected custody limitations, not grounds to fabricate results.
