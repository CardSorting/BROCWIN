# Community code of conduct

Participants should treat one another respectfully, discuss ideas and evidence in good faith, welcome contributors with different backgrounds and abilities, and credit the work they use. Keep technical disagreement focused on claims and behavior.

Harassment, discriminatory attacks, threats, sexualized attention, personal abuse, impersonation and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not publish credentials or private operational data in community discussions.

The institutional steward or a designated moderator may remove harmful content, warn a participant, temporarily restrict participation or exclude a participant, with a proportionate explanation where appropriate. Report a concern privately to an identified project steward through a verified channel. If the concern involves that steward, request an alternate institutional reviewer.

No public moderation contact has been configured in this local preparation. The hosting owner must designate a reachable moderator and private reporting channel before opening community participation. Do not route reports to unrelated upstream projects.

This original project policy applies to project-managed issue trackers, pull requests, discussions and other official community spaces. It does not claim adoption of a third-party covenant or an existing moderation service.
