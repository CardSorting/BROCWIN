# Governance and project status

MAJO, Executive Chair of HEAV3NS, issued the commission and closed the experimental engineering cycle. BROCWIN is currently in documentation and preservation. Engineering can reopen only through an explicit institutional instruction defining scope and acceptance.

A technical steward maintains evidence continuity; that assignment is not exclusive authorship. Contributors retain their rights and attribution. New maintainers, moderators and release owners must be explicitly designated; this preparation assigns no GitHub identities or maintainership permissions.

Changes proceed through a described proposal, a scoped reviewable diff, evidence and license checks, and an accountable maintainer decision. Larger architecture/scope changes, new infrastructure, spending and weaker security controls need separate authority. Conflicting claims are resolved against original records and exact build identities; unresolved findings remain recorded.

No CLA, copyright assignment, paid support scheme or contributor voting structure is introduced. Contributions use the applicable existing licenses and the provenance certification in CONTRIBUTING.md.

Sealed research editions remain immutable. Community-file preparation and correction do not imply full-port completion or authorize runtime changes. A release owner must resolve the decisions in [the open-source release guide](docs/open-source-release.md) before public distribution.
